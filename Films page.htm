Arrietty

借りぐらしのアリエッティ · Karigurashi no Arrietti

2010 / 94 min / Fantasy · Adventure

Hidden beneath the floorboards of an ordinary house, a tiny borrower named Arrietty lives with her family in secret. When an unexpected friendship forms with a human boy, her quiet world begins to change forever.

Directed by Hiromasa Yonebayashi
